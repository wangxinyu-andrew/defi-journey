# Day 05: Liquidation

Liquidation is a mechanism that protects the protocol from bad debt.

When a user's collateral ratio becomes too low, a liquidator can repay part of the user's debt and receive collateral with a bonus.

## Why Liquidators Exist

Liquidators are external actors who help keep the protocol solvent.

## Key Insight

Liquidation is not punishment. It is a risk-control mechanism.
