# Day 02: ERC-20 Notes

ERC-20 is not just a token. It is an interface standard.

## balanceOf

Records how many tokens each address owns.

## transfer

Moves tokens from msg.sender to another address.

## approve

Allows another address or contract to spend tokens on behalf of the owner.

## allowance

Records how much the spender is still allowed to spend.

## transferFrom

Allows the approved spender to move tokens from one address to another.
